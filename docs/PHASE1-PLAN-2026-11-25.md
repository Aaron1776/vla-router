## Pre-stated rules for analyses (i) and (ii) (fixed 2026-11-25 ~06:15Z, before either analysis ran)

Data: the pilot's base points, labels scored under `cascader_nospread` (the stop rule's profile since amendment 63);
positive = p_2 - p_1 >= 1/3. Unscored points (the profile excluded them) count as not positive, as in the re-plan's
pooled rates.

**(i) Where positives fall within failed episodes.** Position = the decision point's index (10 steps each; Phase 1's
frame is indices 0-51). Three stretches fixed in advance: early 0-16, middle 17-33, late 34-51. For each stretch,
its positive rate among failed-parent points against the rest's: two-sided Fisher exact test. The pattern counts as
clear only if (a) some stretch differs at p < 0.05/3 (Bonferroni over the three), and (b) drawing the higher-rate side
at twice the per-point rate of the lower-rate side, within each task's failed stratum, raises the expected effective
positives per fold (below) by at least 10% at the same budget, with each side's rate taken from the pilot. If both
hold, Phase 1 draws that way and every point's inclusion probability records the stretch; otherwise sampling within
the failed stratum stays uniform (amendment 57).

**(ii) Failed-parent share 2/3 versus 3/4.** Per task, round(s x n) points from failed parents, capped by the failed
points available, the shortfall from the other stratum (amendment 57 (2)); weights w = 1/π. Expected positives per
stratum = n x r (pooled rates); a fold's expected effective positives = (Σ w)² / Σ w² over the expected positives of
its 8 training tasks, with each stratum's positives at that stratum's weight. Better = the larger minimum over the ten
folds; within 1%, the larger mean; still within 1%, 2/3 (the approved value).
